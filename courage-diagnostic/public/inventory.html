The Lie Economy Inventory

Thirty-seven statements. Answer them the way things actually are, not the way you have described them to your boss.

This is not a personality test. It is not a development tool designed to make you feel good about your “growth areas.”

Answer with brutal honesty. Not how you wish things were. How they actually are. If you find yourself rationalizing, qualifying, or adding “but” to your answer — that is The Will talking. Pay attention to it.

This inventory is a mirror.

Before you begin

Do not take this alone. Take it, then ask your people to take it about you. Then compare. If there is a significant gap between how you scored yourself and how they scored you, that gap is your Lie Economy — the distance between the story you are telling yourself and the truth.

The gap is the point

The most valuable thing here is not your score. It is the gap between your score and your team’s. That gap is the Lie Economy made visible. If the gap is small, you have something to build on. If it is large, you have something to reckon with. Either way — now you know.

And once you know, you can no longer pretend you don’t know.
